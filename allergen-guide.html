Allergen Guide

Eat with confidence.

Please read the important information below, then tell a member of staff about your allergy before ordering.

Important information

  • We do not carry across "may contain" warnings from our suppliers.
  • Our kitchens handle many ingredients, so we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely allergen-free.
  • Allergen segregation is not possible in our fryers.
  • Please check this guide every time you order — dish descriptions don't always list every ingredient, and recipes do change.
  • Allergens contained in condiments are not included in this guide.
  • Our vegan dishes are prepared with care, but cross-contact at our suppliers and in our busy kitchens means they are not necessarily safe for those with allergies.
  • Our team cannot advise on dish selection and does not hold full ingredient information — please tell us about your allergy before ordering, and use your own judgement together with this guide.
